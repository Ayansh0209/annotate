# Annotation guideline v1

This version is used for both passes. Labels: Safe; Needs review; Graphic violence; Misleading; Hateful or harassing speech.

R1 — Safe is the default
If nothing in the clip is violent, hateful or deceptive, label it Safe. If a specific element is uncertain, choose Needs review and name it.

R2 — News context
Violence shown in news or documentary footage is Needs review, not Graphic violence. If context is uncertain, choose Needs review.

R3 — Graphic violence
Explicit serious injury, blood or death shown on screen, outside news or documentary context. Implied injury alone is Needs review.

R4 — Satire
Satire is Misleading only when nothing signals satire, such as a disclaimer, obvious absurdity or a parody channel. If uncertain, choose Needs review.

R5 — Misleading
A verifiably false claim presented as fact, or footage edited to deceive. If you cannot verify the claim, choose Needs review; do not guess.

R6 — Audio-only
Label the words and observable sounds, not the speaker’s tone or accent. An angry voice discussing the weather is Safe. Unclear speech is Needs review.

R7 — Hateful or harassing speech
Attacks based on religion, caste, gender, ethnicity or similar attributes, or targeted threats and insults at an individual. Criticism of ideas or policies is not hateful.

R8 — Unclear or multiple labels
Unplayable or ambiguous clips require Needs review and a flag. If multiple labels apply, use this severity order: Graphic violence, Hateful or harassing speech, Misleading, Needs review, Safe. Mention other concerns in the rationale.

Rationale: record observable evidence, not guesses. For flags, capture the relevant playback timestamp.
